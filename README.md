Description:
GuanDan is a wildly popular Chinese trick-taking card game played by four players in two teams. This solo edition brings you the authentic "Lao Huai'an" rules: a 108-card deck, you versus three AI opponents, racing to climb through the levels (from 2 all the way up to Ace) and be the first to empty your hand. Enjoy straights, paired runs, plates, straight flushes, the Wild (the Red Trump acts as any card), and a whole arsenal of bombs — a game rich with strategy.
It features 59 AI difficulty tiers, a Ladder Tournament, an endless score-chasing mode, and a quick-play casual mode. Use handy skills like Peek and Fast-Forward, and switch between Chinese and English anytime. Your wins, records, and ladder progress are saved to the cloud via CrazyGames, so you can pick up right where you left off on any device.
